## Google Sans

`google-sans.woff2` uses Google Sans v14.000 from the [official Google Fonts release](https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans/releases/tag/v14.000), retrieved on 10 October 2026. The [release archive](https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans/releases/download/v14.000/GoogleSans-v14.000.zip) has SHA-256 `d338f0e74f5eabead86a85b87edf4cc50cd6561196dbe173d1db4139e95b3ca7`.

The web font was derived from the release's Google Sans variable font using FontTools subsetting and WOFF2 encoding. It retains the Google Sans family identity, names, layout features and variable axes. Its SHA-256 is `f164f3150057c764d05660963145ae40761245cb8f8a8fb61d4d00655d2dd710`.

Unicode subset: U+0000–00FF, U+0100–024F, U+1E00–1EFF, U+2000–206F, U+20A0–20CF, U+2100–214F, U+2190–21FF, U+2212, U+FEFF, U+FFFD.
