What is Chris AI?

Chris AI by DreamPulze is a personal AI chief of staff. Its approach brings together a situation brief, a comparison of options and a practical first task, agenda or draft plan.

Who is it for?

The examples here focus on founders preparing investor updates, leaders making team decisions and independent professionals working through client scope.

Are these live product results?

These are authored, illustrative examples of the approach. The controls switch between prepared examples locally; they do not contact an AI model. The photographs are AI-generated brand imagery, rather than customer portraits.

Does Chris make the decision for me?

You review the evidence, assumptions and proposed plan before taking action. Chris helps prepare the thinking; your judgment remains part of the decision.
